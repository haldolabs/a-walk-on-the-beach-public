# a walk on the beach — pictures

Screenshots of [*a walk on the beach*](https://synapticon.itch.io/beach-walk), kept here so the
itch.io page can show them. The game's source is in a private repository; this one holds only
images, one short film, and the occasional note from the bunker, such as
[`HOT_SAND.md`](HOT_SAND.md): what the sand on the beach and the sand in the machines are
both for.

**The emo otter, walking, thinking.** this hot sand I'm walking on. the thing you're reading
this on is made of it. somebody took
a few grains, melted them, grew them into a crystal and taught them to count. that's a chip.
sand that got a job.

not many got one. for every grain they took, about a million are still here under my feet.
nobody asked these grains either.

they make more every year, twice as many every ten. people draw that line out and say one
day the thinking sand outweighs the beach. lines like that never get there. they go up and
up and then one day they just don't, and nobody announces it. the sand that got a job gives
up before the sand that didn't.

or maybe it doesn't give up. maybe it's doing the same job in there that it does out here.
sand wears things away. that's what it's for, if it's for anything. footprints, castles,
names written with a stick, the memory of whoever wrote them. the thinking sand does it too,
only faster: every copy of a copy drops a word, a million machines each misremembering a poet
by a little, and by the end it's slop, warm and smooth and nobody's. the telephone game,
played by the sand itself, for whatever comes after us to read. the sands of time didn't need
a job. they were already doing it.

I find that weirdly comforting. the water's warm. I might stand in it for a bit.

standing here, I can see what churns this sand. it's grit, worked over by the crashing waves
and by the tide, pulled in and out by our loving satellite. so when sand becomes a thinking
machine, what's the churning force in there? who is the wave crashing?

`film/emo-otter.mp4` (and the same as a silent `.gif`) is ten seconds of the otter on the
beach at sunset: a generated film, redrawn frame by frame in the game's own painted look, with
the game's synthesised surf as its sound.
